History & purpose

Opened on the Balearics with its roots in Marstrand, Halyard Palma comes from a simple observation: most classic yachts here are sailed too little, varnished too late and paid for twice.

Between marinas that hand every risk to the owner and charter docks that wear boats out in three summers, we built a third way: fewer yachts, longer stays, steady care.

We act as a trusted hand between owners and the people who sail their boats, adding to the hull’s value while keeping its accounts in order.

Our three working principles

The rigour of a working yard

We look after yachts on behalf of exacting owners: families, estates, foundations and private sailors. Each mandate has its own technical file, full compliance with flag and charter rules, and a clear monthly report.

Roots in two sailing harbours

The quality of our charters rests on the people who sail them. In Palma we work with schools, skippers and returning families; in Marstrand, with owners who lend us their trust and, every so often, their dogs.

All trades under one roof

We do not hand your yacht to strangers. Our own crews oversee fit-out, inventories, and the preventive and corrective work each hull needs, week after week.
