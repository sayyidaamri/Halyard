The annual plan,
done by hand

Designed for owners who live far from the quay, sail when they can and expect to find varnish dry, batteries full and sails bent on, season care covers the twelve months of a wooden yacht’s life.

It suits those who would rather spend August at the helm than on the telephone, and who want the yearly cost settled in advance.

Isolde

Marstrand, inner harbour

A varnished cutter from 1951, kept by Halyard since 2017. Isolde spends her winters indoors, under a fitted cover, and comes out every April with a fresh coat on her brightwork. Her owners step aboard perhaps forty days a year and have not opened a toolbox since. A fine picture of what season care is for.
